The title was the idea

“The idea came to me from the title of the challenge itself. You could make a lot of things from that title, but the title itself gave me the idea to make a game like this.”

P0021 / 2026-11-14 17:36 / Gulf time
Translated from Persian, excerpt

WHAT I DECIDED

I found the jam with about two days left. The theme, Director's Cut, suggested a director who fixes a stunt and shoots again.

WHO DID WHAT

I directed and playtested. I wrote no code. Claude wrote the game in Claude Code. My job was to decide whether the result was worth playing.

One jump was not a game

“Look, what it had made was very, very simple. It had completely forgotten this was supposed to be a game that was fun for the person playing it.”

P0016 / 2026-11-14 10:39 / Gulf time
Translated from Persian, excerpt

The log records my rejection at 10:15: one leap and two lettered spots for a mat. There was too little to do.

Keep the engine work. Start the game layer again. I wanted several parkour moves, a moving car and pieces I could drag freely. The surviving message is stamped 10:39; the log uses 10:15.

ACT II / THE RULECUT! AGAIN! / GLASS BOX

Lucky reacts to the set

“This time make me an interesting game, one that is interesting to watch, where you can easily adjust the moves with the mouse, call cut, run it again and finally see the result.”

P0016 / 2026-11-14 10:39 / Gulf time
Translated from Persian, excerpt

WHAT THE AGENT DID

At 10:40, Lucky stopped following a fixed script. He leaps at an edge, tries a traversal move at a climbable surface, slides under low things and gets hurt by the rest.

WHAT THE TEST SHOWED

He runs at 5.9 m/s. A level leap clears 5.3 m. The sample's ledge catch reaches about 7.5 m. That unplanned catch became the first beat.

ACT III / THE SOUNDCUT! AGAIN! / GLASS BOX

The piano had to go

“The music in the final video is really bad. This is an action scene. It needs action music.”

P0018 / 2026-11-14 14:58 / Gulf time
Translated from Persian, excerpt

WHAT I DECIDED

The quiet piano did not fit the stunt. I wanted action music, support for slow motion and a different feel for the wrecks.

WHAT THE AGENT DID

ElevenLabs made new chase, film, outtake and set-up tracks. The agent measured the take track near 138 BPM and the outtakes near 146. It did not listen. Only I heard the game.

ACT III / THE BILLCUT! AGAIN! / GLASS BOX

Every cut should cost

“I think every cut should cost us filming and actor fees. So if someone cuts five times before getting there, their film costs more to make. That counts against them.”

P0018 / 2026-11-14 14:58 / Gulf time
Translated from Persian, excerpt

WHAT I DECIDED

A cleaner take should earn more, too. Two successful ledge grabs should not pay the same if one puts his hand through the wall.

WHAT THE AGENT DID

A take costs $20,000 plus wreck damage. The game measures each move, turns quality into box office and subtracts the bill. At 15:45 the world board followed.

14 NOV / Logs 41-42, 15:30-15:45 / P0018 14:58 / Costs checked against project handoff / Read log entry09 / 24
ACT III / THE BODYCUT! AGAIN! / GLASS BOX

He should hit the ground

“Why is the animation like this when the car hits him? He should be thrown to the ground.”

P0020 / 2026-11-14 17:25 / Gulf time
Translated from Persian, excerpt

WHAT THE TEST SHOWED

The push was not the problem. The sample's active ragdoll drove the limbs toward a pose and held him upright.

WHAT THE AGENT DID

It turned those controls off at a vehicle hit and threw the whole body. Measured throws went 6.5 m for the car and 8.5 m for the truck. Sunday would expose the same mistake in a fall.

ACT IV / A FILM TO KEEPCUT! AGAIN! / GLASS BOX

Let the player save it

“At the end, the player should be able to save the final videos separately: the last clean cut, and a video of the bugs and crashes with good music.”

P0020 / 2026-11-14 17:25 / Gulf time
Translated from Persian, excerpt

WHAT THE AGENT DID

At 19:15 the game could render MP4s offline. It replays stored poses, captures frames and logs sounds. A mixer rebuilds the audio; FFmpeg encodes it.

WHAT I DECIDED

The final format became a name card, the clean cut, BACKSTAGE, wrecks separated by colour bars and a short beep, then rolling credits. Wide film, tall film and tall shorts.

ACT V / SUNDAY, 01:03CUT! AGAIN! / GLASS BOX

Why is the saved film dark?

“Why are all these scenes the player exports to share on social media so dark? I attached an example.”

P0022 / 2026-11-15 01:03 / Gulf time
Translated from Persian, excerpt

WHAT THE AGENT FOUND

The export used the wrong gamma. Lumen and the real-time sky light also did not run through the capture camera.

WHAT THE TEST SHOWED

A 2.2 gamma target, one sky-light capture and screen-space bounce light brought the mean to 1.4% below the screen. The darkest tenth measured 28 against 24. H11 followed.

15 NOV / Logs 53 and 57, 01:25 and 02:40 / P0022 01:03 / Same-frame comparison / Read log entry12 / 24
ACT V / SUNDAY, 01:03CUT! AGAIN! / GLASS BOX

The letters were being stretched

“Why is the font quality so low? In some places, anyway.”

P0022 / 2026-11-15 01:03 / Gulf time
Translated from Persian, excerpt

WHAT THE AGENT FOUND

A 28 pixel line at 1080p was a 20 pixel font enlarged by half again. The text could pass a content check and still look soft.

WHAT THE AGENT DID

It rasterised text at the displayed size and placed it on whole pixels. The log reports twenty times as many hard edges in the instruction line on a full-size frame.

ACT V / SUNDAY, 05:50CUT! AGAIN! / GLASS BOX

I followed the hint. He crashed.

“Point six: fix the text position, but what I meant was, when I line up the gap in that blue box with the orange box on the ground, it goes wrong! Test it yourself if you want. Maybe I am wrong.”

P0026 / 2026-11-15 05:50 / Gulf time
Translated from Persian

WHAT THE TEST SHOWED

I was right. The blue mark was 5 m long; the box was 2.2 m. Their overlap allowed a start 1.5 m too early. The slide only worked from 1.3 m too early.

WHAT THE AGENT DID

Sweeps measured the usable window for the car and truck. Both marks now have that length. The window edges passed in both scenes.

ACT V / THE LIGHTCUT! AGAIN! / GLASS BOX

I needed to see Lucky

“The lighting angle is not good. For example, here at the end when he stops, his body is in shadow.”

P0025 / 2026-11-15 05:14 / Gulf time
Translated from Persian, excerpt

WHAT THE AGENT DID

A camera-side fill light lit Lucky without adding shadows. The export also received a warm film grade. The wall lettering moved onto solid wall where a gate had cut it.

WHAT THE TEST SHOWED

The graded export measured 4% brighter on average than the matching screen frame, with more open shadows. That is a measurement, not my claim that every shot was finished.

Nobody hit him. Why ask?

“Why is the director asking if Lucky is all right here, in the picture I sent? This is not a wreck scene!”

P0027 / 2026-11-15 06:59 / Gulf time
Translated from Persian, excerpt

WHAT MY TEST SHOWED

The car had never arrived. Lucky had not been hit, but the director was checking whether he was hurt. A line picked from one list did not know the difference.

It grouped lines by outcome. Hits and falls can get concern. A missed vehicle gets another take. The distinction applies in play and in the saved films.

“At the start of the game his feet are not on the ground. If you need to make another version of the game for any reason, fix this.”

P0028 / 2026-11-15 07:03 / Gulf time

WHAT THE AGENT FOUND

Lucky waited 14 cm above the roof. The sample pawn never settled while waiting, so the agent placed him on the floor at spawn.

What I called lag

“Look at all the frames between seconds 4 and 5 of this video. It lags several times. I have seen it in lots of other places in the videos and the game too. It needs fixing at the root.”

P0029 / 2026-11-15 07:19 / Gulf time
Translated from Persian, excerpt

WHAT THE AGENT FOUND

Across 28 wrecked takes and 6,832 limp frames, the recording was clean. Replays mixed poses in mesh space while the capsule turned half round.

Blending in world space removed the flash. The logged follow-up covered 22 wrecked takes and 14 saved videos, with no flash. A separate first-frame skeleton detail bug was fixed too.

ACT VI / TEST AND REPORTCUT! AGAIN! / GLASS BOX

The tester could only test

“Maybe the old version of the game was running. Anyway, yes, test the problems you fixed, and finish all the trailers and videos.”

P0032 / 2026-11-15 08:11 / Gulf time
Translated from Persian

HOW I WORKED

The planner wrote briefs for fresh builder sessions. A separate tester could run and report, but could not fix. It found bugs the builder missed twice. Its H13 pass began about 08:50.

WHAT THAT DID NOT PROVE

Scripted input checked repeatable behaviour. Pictures checked the result. Neither proved the sound was good. H14 had a smoke test and my own play, not a full independent Shipping pass.

The build that went in

“Okay, it is uploaded. Now how do we send it to Epic?”

P0050 / 2026-11-15 12:49 / Gulf time
Translated from Persian

WHAT I SUBMITTED

H14, a 557 MB zip against the 1 GB limit. The first 3D Shipping zip had been 1.07 GB. Trimming unused sample characters made room for the game and its video encoder.

About 13:00 on Sunday, after about 30 hours from Saturday 07:05. Three scenes and ten kinds of wreck. The submission was about 17 hours before the deadline.

“Leave it.”
